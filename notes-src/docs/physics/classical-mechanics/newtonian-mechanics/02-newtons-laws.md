# 2. Newton's Laws

## The three laws

1. In an inertial frame, a body with no net force moves with constant velocity.
2. $\mathbf{F} = \dfrac{d\mathbf{p}}{dt}$, which for constant mass is $\mathbf{F} = m\mathbf{a}$.
3. Forces between two bodies are equal and opposite: $\mathbf{F}_{12} = -\mathbf{F}_{21}$.

## Momentum conservation

!!! abstract "Theorem"
    For an isolated system of particles, the total momentum
    $\mathbf{P} = \sum_i m_i \mathbf{v}_i$ is conserved.

??? proof "Proof"
    Summing the second law over all particles, internal forces cancel in pairs by
    the third law, so $\dot{\mathbf{P}} = \mathbf{F}_{\text{ext}} = 0$.
