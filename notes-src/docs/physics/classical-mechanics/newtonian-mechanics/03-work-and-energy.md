# 3. Work and Energy

## Work–energy theorem

The work done along a path $C$ is $W = \int_C \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r}$, and

$$
W = \Delta T, \qquad T = \tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 .
$$

## Conservative forces

A force is conservative when $\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = 0$ on a simply connected
domain. Then $\mathbf{F} = -\nabla V$ and $E = T + V$ is conserved.
