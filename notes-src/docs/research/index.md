# Research Notes

Notes for my research in QCD thermodynamics and the effective models used to study it.
Each model is split into courses, one for each form of the model, and each course into chapters.

## [QCD Thermodynamics](qcd-thermodynamics/index.md)

The thermodynamics of strongly interacting matter: the common ground the models below are built to describe and are tested against.

- [The QCD Phase Diagram](qcd-thermodynamics/phase-diagram.md)
- [Lattice QCD Results](qcd-thermodynamics/lattice-qcd.md)
- [Fluctuations of Conserved Charges](qcd-thermodynamics/fluctuations.md)

## [Hadron Resonance Gas](hrg/index.md)

Below the transition, QCD thermodynamics is well described by a gas of hadrons and their resonances. Each course below is one form of the model.

- [Ideal HRG](hrg/ideal-hrg.md)
- [Resonance Decays](hrg/resonance-decays.md)
- [Excluded-Volume HRG](hrg/excluded-volume-hrg.md)
- [Van der Waals HRG](hrg/van-der-waals-hrg.md)

## [Nambu–Jona-Lasinio Model](njl/index.md)

An effective model of quarks with a four-fermion interaction that captures dynamical chiral symmetry breaking. Each course below is one form of the model.

- [NJL Model](njl/njl-model.md)
- [Polyakov-loop NJL](njl/pnjl.md)
- [Non-Local NJL](njl/non-local-njl.md)
- [Magnetic Field NJL](njl/magnetic-field-njl.md)

## [Quark-Meson Model](quark-meson/index.md)

Quarks coupled to mesonic fields, a renormalizable alternative to the NJL model. Each course below is one form of the model.
